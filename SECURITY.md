# Security policy

If you find a security problem in this repository, please tell us privately first.

**How to report:** use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** button (Security tab of this repository), or email goncalo@goncaloesteves.io with the subject "Security report". Say what you found, where, and how to see it. Please don't open a public issue or share it publicly until it is fixed.

**What we promise:** a reply within 5 business days, updates until it is fixed, and credit when it is fixed if you want to be named. If you act in good faith, stay within this repository and our own sites, and stop as soon as you have shown the problem, we will not take legal action against you.

**Not in scope:** third-party services, denial of service, social engineering, and automated scanner output with no shown impact.

Full policy: https://goncaloesteves.io/security/ · Descoberta Systems LLC
