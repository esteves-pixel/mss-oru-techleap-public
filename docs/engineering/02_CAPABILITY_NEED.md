# 02 — High-Level Capability Need

## Statement

**Need:** Continuous, **repositionable** external sensing and early anomaly detection for robotic and modular space systems, reducing dependence on EVAs and offline / opportunistic inspection.

## Problem evidence (operational)

Long-lived robotic systems accumulate wear, joint anomalies, end-effector degradation, and external environment damage. Canadarm2’s extended life and service events (joint failure requiring EVA, LEE replacements, micrometeoroid strike) show that:

1. Design life and inspection cadence can diverge from real operations.  
2. Failures and damage still escalate to **human EVA** or major logistics.  
3. Ground-heavy ops limit responsiveness; future systems need more **onboard sensing + autonomy**.  

## Capability gap (ISAM)

| Today | Gap | Desired |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Periodic visual/EVA inspection | Sparse temporal coverage | Continuous external monitoring |
| Fixed sensors on host | Cannot re-task geometry easily | Robotically repositionable sensor ORU |
| Offline analysis | Delayed anomaly recognition | Edge features + early flags |
| Replace whole assemblies | High logistics cost | Modular ORU swap / relocate |

## Why robotic manipulation is essential

A fixed “sensing box” bolted once does **not** close the gap. The capability is:

- Place monitoring where risk appears  
- Reorient for multi-aspect coverage  
- Remove / replace without EVA  
- Demonstrate modular logistics for ISAM  

Without the FFR arm, MSS-ORU cannot realize reconfiguration, transfer from delivery vehicle, or multi-site demonstration. Manipulation is **the** enabler of value.

## Beneficiaries

- Long-duration robotic arms and dexterous manipulators  
- Modular platforms and observatories (SF27 / SF28 class needs)  
- Operators seeking fewer EVAs for inspect/maintain tasks  
- Future commercial ISAM service providers  

## Success at field level (not just this flight)

If proven, the field gains a pattern: **robotically installed external health-monitoring ORUs** as a standard ISAM building block — analogous to modular compute ORUs, but for **physical state awareness**.
